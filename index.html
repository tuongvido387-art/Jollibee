What it is

A little less “what should we get?”

Jolly Guide is a simple meal-planning companion inspired by Jollibee’s familiar mix of fried chicken, burgers, spaghetti, sides, and sweet pies. It helps you organize the choices you already love around the people, appetite, and moment you have in mind.

Think of it as a starting point for your order—not an ordering service. Check the current local menu for portions, prices, ingredients, and availability.

Where it saves a little time

Less scrolling. More table talk.

Small planning moments can make getting everyone’s favorites on the same page feel simple.

A helpful guide stays honest

What it can—and can’t—do.

Jolly Guide is here to help organize a meal idea, while leaving real-world details for you to confirm.

✓ It can help with

  • Organize menu favorites into a readable lineup.
  • Suggest a flexible mix based on your occasion and group size.
  • Keep sides and dessert ideas from getting forgotten.
  • Point you to Jollibee’s menu for current local details.

✕ It cannot do

  • Place, pay for, or change an order.
  • Guarantee current menu availability, prices, portions, or promotions.
  • Confirm allergy safety; use official local ingredient and allergen information.
  • Claim to be an official Jollibee service or represent the company.
How to use the guide

Start with a craving. Add the people.

Use the builder above, then check the official local menu before deciding. Share only the details you need to make a meal plan.

Try this
“Help me plan a Jollibee meal for four. We’d like Chickenjoy and Jolly Spaghetti, and I want a couple of sides and a dessert idea. Keep it flexible and remind me what to verify.”

A few things to keep in mind

  • Menu items, serving sizes, prices, and promotions can vary by location and change over time.
  • Check with the local restaurant about ingredients, allergens, and dietary needs.
  • Use Jollibee’s official channels for current menu and ordering details.
  • This is an independent fan-made concept and is not sponsored by or affiliated with Jollibee.
